Zero Intermediary Architecture

Bypassing the URL Shortener Trap

Most commercial QR platforms act as cloaked redirect proxies. They encode a short link (e.g. qr.vendor.io/xyz) that routes to their servers before redirecting to your destination.

The moment you stop paying their monthly subscription, they redirect your printed codes to paywalls or 404 pages. QiCraft static codes contain the final endpoint or text directly, making them physically immune to shutdowns, acquisitions, rate limits, or billing disputes.

Reed-Solomon Level H allocates 30% of the entire grid payload to redundancy polynomials. This serves two vital real-world functions:

  • Emblem & Logo Insertion: You can position custom brand logos over the center of the code without breaking scannability because the remaining 70% of the code reconstructs all original data bytes.
  • Industrial Resistance: Physical signage, outdoor posters, and product packaging subject to scratches, folds, rain, or glare continue scanning effortlessly even with nearly a third of the print obscured.
